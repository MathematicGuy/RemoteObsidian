# Biological Neural Networks

Biological neural networks form the basis of animal central nervous systems. This note details the structure of biological neurons, synaptic transmission, and long-term potentiation.

## Neuron Structure
A biological neuron consists of dendrites, a soma (cell body), and a single axon. Electrical impulses (action potentials) travel from the dendrites, through the soma, and down the axon.

## Synaptic Transmission
When an action potential reaches the axon terminal, it triggers the release of chemical neurotransmitters across the synaptic cleft to bind to receptors on the postsynaptic dendrite.

## Long-Term Potentiation (LTP)
LTP is a persistent strengthening of synapses based on recent patterns of activity, serving as the primary cellular mechanism behind learning and memory consolidation (neuroplasticity).
