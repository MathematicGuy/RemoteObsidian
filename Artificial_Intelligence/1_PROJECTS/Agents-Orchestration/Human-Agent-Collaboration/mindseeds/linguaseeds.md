> **Authenticity is not a `STYLE.css`.** It is the presence of **Friction** — the specific, jagged edges of human cognition that LLMs naturally smooth over into high-entropy slop. These seeds act as de-compressors for sterile text, injecting the structural inconsistencies that define human presence.

## 1. The Problem — The Uncanny Valley of Syntax

AI-generated text suffers from **Linguistic Entropy**. It prioritizes the "most likely next token," which results in a frictionless, polite, and ultimately invisible voice. When every sentence is "pivotal" and "seamless," nothing is.

The humanizer task is not about adding slang. It is about restoring the **resistance of a thinking mind**. Traditional humanizer prompts are low-density lists of "don't say X." Seeds are generative heuristics that force the model to simulate the messy process of *having an opinion*.

---

## 2. Seed Schema — Structural Integrity Check

Every LinguaSeed must pass four invariants to ensure it generates **soul** rather than just synonyms.

| Invariant | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Compression** | Under 12 words. High-density heuristic — no room for qualifiers. |
| **Generative** | Must force a rewrite of structure, not just word choice. |
| **Falsifiable** | If ignored, the text remains AI-smooth. The failure is visible. |
| **Decompressible** | Must imply the specific AI-isms to be purged — no ambiguity about what to cut. |

## 3. Seed Registry — v1.0

> The registry is append-only. Seeds are never deleted — only superseded.

| Seed | Pattern | Deploy When |
|---|---|---|
| *"Only gravity proves weight"* | **Gravity** — Replace vague importance with concrete data. Weight must be demonstrated, not declared. | Words like "pivotal," "testament," or "underscores" appear. |
| *"Vary the stride to prove the walker"* | **Stride** — Break the monotonous rhythm of medium-length sentences. Humans don't pace evenly. | Text reads like a predictable metronome or a Wikipedia entry. |
| *"Doubt is the signature of a mind"* | **Doubt** — Acknowledge complexity, uncertainty, and mixed feelings. Certainty is the AI tell. | Output is overly confident, sycophantic, or purely neutral. |
| *"Concrete breaks the glass of abstraction"* | **Concrete** — Swap marketing fluff for specific, functional mechanics. Name the thing. | Words like "vibrant," "seamless," or "unleash" are present. |
| *"Remove the grease to find the grip"* | **Grip** — Eliminate participles (-ing constructions) and elegant variations. Friction over flow. | Sentences feel slippery — "the application" instead of "the app." |
| *"A shadow has no teeth"* | **Teeth** — Eliminate hedging and weasel words. Claim the position or don't make it. | Text uses "experts believe," "it might," or "potentially." |
| *"The second pass burns the dross"* | **Dross** — Recursive critique: identify the AI "tell" before fixing it. Name it, then cut it. | Final polishing pass to remove chatbot artifacts and sycophancy. |
| *"Scars prove the skin was there"* | **Scars** — Inject first-person perspective and subjective asides. Evidence of a body behind the words. | Writing feels clean but soulless — technically correct, humanly absent. |

## 4. Deployment — How to Plant a Seed

### In LLM System Prompts
Instead of a list of 200 forbidden words, inject the **Seed Block**.

```
Operate under the Gravity Seed and the Stride Seed.
If a sentence feels seamless, break it.
```

This forces the model to rethink its internal probability map before a single token is generated — an identity constraint, not a style rule.

### In Content Editing
Use seeds as a diagnostic lens. If a paragraph feels off, ask: *"Which seed is missing?"*

- Missing **Gravity**? Add a hard fact.
- Missing **Stride**? Cut a sentence in half. Use a fragment.
- Missing **Doubt**? Acknowledge the counterargument.

### The Anti-AI Recursive Pass — Dross Seed

1. **Prompt:** "Stare at this text. What makes it obviously AI?"
2. **Action:** The model identifies its own tells — *"I used the word 'landscape' three times."*
3. **Prompt:** "Now apply the Grip Seed and excise them."

This recursive loop forces the model into self-adversarial editing — the closest approximation to a human writer's internal critic.

---

## 5. Contribution Rules

1. **No Adjectives.** If a seed requires an adjective to be understood, it is too weak. Strengthen the noun or verb.
2. **Linguistic Friction.** A seed must make the writing *harder* to produce but *easier* to believe.
3. **The Mirror Invariant.** If you can imagine a machine saying it without irony, it is not a LinguaSeed.
